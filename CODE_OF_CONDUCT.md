# Code of Conduct

## Our Goal
**Buzzard-01** is an open-source hobby project focused on model rocketry, 3D printing, and custom avionics. Our goal is to foster an open, welcoming, and constructive environment for makers, engineers, and enthusiasts of all skill levels.

## Expected Behavior
* **Be respectful and constructive:** Offer helpful feedback on rocket designs, PCB layouts, and code. Everyone starts somewhere.
* **Share knowledge openly:** If you spot a flaw in the OpenRocket simulation, KiCad schematics, or MicroPython code, explain why and suggest improvements.
* **Prioritize safety and legality:** Model rocketry involves energetic materials and airspace rules. Discussions, modifications, and build advice must strictly adhere to local laws and established model rocketry safety codes (such as NAR or Tripoli standards).

## Unacceptable Behavior
* Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal attacks of any kind.
* Sharing or encouraging dangerous, illegal, or reckless modifications (e.g., homemade propellants without proper certification, launching near airports/crowds, or weaponization).
* Dismissing beginners or gatekeeping engineering/maker topics.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Unacceptable behavior may result in comment deletion, issue locking, or blocking from the repository.

If you observe unacceptable behavior or safety concerns, please report them by contacting the project maintainer at **maximmilian.linus.foerster@gmail.com** or by opening a private security/moderation inquiry.
